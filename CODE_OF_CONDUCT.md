# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment for contributors
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
sexual identity or orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
* Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and the work.
* Accept review and security feedback professionally.
* Respect contributor privacy and handle vulnerability information responsibly.
* Do not publish credentials, private keys, exploit details, or sensitive
  production data.
* Do not harass, threaten, use discriminatory language, give unwanted sexual
  attention, dox others, or sustain disruption.

## Enforcement

For harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, or other behaviour that
violates GitHub's platform rules, use GitHub's built-in reporting and blocking
controls.

For a project-specific conduct concern needing maintainer attention, open a
minimal GitHub issue requesting follow-up. Do not include sensitive details,
personal information, credentials, exploit details, or other private material
in that public issue.

Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never
through a public conduct issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation where necessary.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.
